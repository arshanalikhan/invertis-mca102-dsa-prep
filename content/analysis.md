# What repeats in Data Structures papers

**Why AKTU papers?** Invertis University does not put its MCA102 question papers online (searches of the university site and the usual paper archives found only its syllabus). The closest public papers are AKTU's **MCA Data Structures** papers. The match is very close: the Invertis MCA102 units (arrays, stacks and queues, linked lists, trees, searching/sorting/search trees/hashing, graphs) are the same topics as AKTU's RCA202 and NMCA213 syllabi, and KCA205/BMC205 add only a few algorithm-design topics. Both universities set **CO-based papers with Sections A, B and C**. Every question from the 8 papers was tagged with the MCA102 topic it belongs to; the full list is on the [past papers](papers.html) page.

## The paper pattern

| Section | AKTU MCA (70-mark papers) | Choice | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 7 short questions, 2 marks each | Attempt all | 14 |
| B | 5 questions, 7 marks each | Attempt any 3 | 21 |
| C | Q3 to Q7, each with parts (a) and (b), 7 marks | Attempt 1 part of each | 35 |
| | 3 hours | | **70** |

The Invertis MCA102 scheme also has a **70-mark end-semester exam** (plus Mid Term 12, Teacher's Assessment 6 and Attendance 12 = 100). Invertis papers are CO-based like AKTU's, so expect every unit to be covered. The 100-mark AKTU papers (KCA205, NMCA213) have 10 short questions and 10-mark long questions but the same three sections.

> **Every unit is examined.** In the CO-based papers each Section C question comes from one course outcome (unit), so no unit can be skipped. With six units in MCA102, expect at least one compulsory choice from each.

## Questions that repeat almost word for word

- **"What is AVL tree? Explain the balancing methods of AVL trees with an example"**: identical in RCA202 2018-19 Q2c and NMCA213 2018-19 Q2d; AVL rotations again in 2017-18, 2022-23 and 2023-24.
- **"What are the different ways the graph is represented in computer memory? Explain with suitable example"**: identical in RCA202 2016-17 Q2a and 2017-18 Q7a; graph representation again in 2018-19 and 2022-23.
- **"Write a program for insertion sorting. Analyze its running time"**: identical in 2016-17 Q2g and 2017-18 Q1g; insertion sort again in 2017-18 NMCA213, 2018-19 and 2024-25.
- **Singly linked list: "insert a node after a given node" and "delete the last node"**: 2018-19 RCA202 Q4b and 2022-23 KCA205 Q3b, same two operations.
- **Postfix evaluation algorithm with a stack**: 2018-19 NMCA213, 2022-23 and 2023-24.
- **Infix to postfix algorithm**: both 2017-18 papers; conversions also in 2016-17 and 2018-19.
- **B-tree of order 3 construction**: 2017-18, 2023-24 and 2024-25.
- **Polynomial addition using linked lists**: both 2018-19 papers and 2023-24.
- **Dijkstra's algorithm**: 2016-17, 2023-24 and 2024-25.
- **Sparse matrix and its representation**: both 2017-18 papers, both 2018-19 papers and 2023-24.
- **Hashing and collision resolution**: 7 of the 8 papers.

## What to expect in MCA102

- **Sorting is the biggest single topic**: quick sort, merge sort and heap sort appear in every paper, often as a trace on given numbers. Insertion and selection sort traces come as short questions.
- **Stack applications are near-certain**: infix → postfix/prefix, postfix evaluation and Tower of Hanoi.
- **Trees**: construct a tree from inorder + preorder, write the three traversals, BST construction from a list of keys, AVL rotations and B-tree insertion.
- **Graphs**: BFS/DFS order, Prim or Kruskal MST and Dijkstra on a given graph.
- **Not in MCA102**: the newer AKTU papers (KCA205, BMC205) add dynamic programming, LCS and Strassen's multiplication; these are tagged "not in syllabus" and are not part of the notes.
- **MCA102 topics the AKTU papers rarely test**: Huffman algorithm, generalized linked lists, adjacency multilists, Warshall's algorithm and activity networks. Invertis teachers may still ask them, so the notes cover them.
